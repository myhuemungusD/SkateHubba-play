## Open Questions for the Owner (do not guess — decide before submitting)

1. **Mapbox GL telemetry.** `vercel.json` CSP allowlists `events.mapbox.com`,
   which is mapbox-gl's telemetry endpoint; the repo never disables Mapbox
   telemetry. Mapbox may receive coarse usage/session data (and tile requests
   inherently reveal the map viewport) when the map is open. Decide: (a)
   disable telemetry and keep "Location: Not collected", or (b) keep it and
   declare approximate location / usage data collected by a third party in
   both consoles. This is the only item blocking a clean "Location: Not
   collected" answer.
2. **Sentry is not consent-gated** (`src/main.tsx` inits whenever
   `VITE_SENTRY_DSN` is set, before any banner). That is common and generally
   acceptable for diagnostics, but it means crash/diagnostic collection must
   be declared as non-optional in the Play form (drafted that way above).
   Confirm you're comfortable, or gate it behind consent.
3. **Are FNV-hashed uids "linked to identity"?** `hashUid`/`hashIdentity`
   (`src/utils/pii.ts`) are deterministic, non-cryptographic hashes the
   developer could re-derive from a uid. Draft answers say "Not linked" for
   Usage Data and Diagnostics on the basis that no raw identifier leaves the
   device and `identify()` is never called — but Apple could view a
   re-derivable hash as linked. Declaring "Linked: Yes" is the zero-risk
   option. Owner's call; both consoles accept either.
4. **Vercel Analytics on native builds.** `<Analytics />`/`<SpeedInsights />`
   load from the serving origin; confirm whether the Capacitor shell serves
   the Vercel-hosted bundle (analytics active on native) or a local bundle
   (web-only). Affects nothing in the draft — both are declared — but worth
   knowing for the Play "optional" framing.
5. **Processor DPAs.** Confirm signed data-processing agreements with Google
   (Firebase), Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), PostHog, Vercel, and
   Mapbox so the "shared: none / processors only" position holds.

### Avatar upload (shipped)

A signed-in user can upload a profile picture from camera or photo library.
The image is screened on-device by NSFWjs before upload and stored at
`users/{uid}/avatar.{webp|jpeg|png}` (≤ 2 MB), served via
`firebasestorage.googleapis.com`.

**iOS permission strings** — verbatim, as they appear in `Info.plist`:

- `NSCameraUsageDescription` — "SkateHubba needs camera access to record your
  tricks, capture skate spots, and take a profile picture."
- `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` — "SkateHubba saves your recorded clips to
  your library and lets you choose a profile picture from your photos."

**Android permissions** — both already declared for trick recording; the
avatar feature required no manifest change:

- `android.permission.CAMERA`
- `android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`

### Native rebuild determination

Avatar upload did **not** require an iOS or Android binary change beyond the
plist copy update. The `@capacitor/camera` plugin registers JavaScript
bindings only; native runtime support for camera and photo library was already
present from the trick-recording feature. Run `npx cap sync` only if a native
rebuild is queued for another reason.
