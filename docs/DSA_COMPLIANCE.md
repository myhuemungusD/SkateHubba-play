# EU distribution and trader verification

**Reconciled 2026-10-26.** These are repository-recorded statuses, not a fresh inspection of Apple, Google, D&B or DNS. D-U-N-S is recorded complete; Apple organization migration is recorded in progress. Do not restart either process based on older checklists.

Apple's EU trader enforcement date was **17 February 2025**, not 2026. Trader verification is an EU distribution requirement, and trader status must be declared during submission. It is not the same as organization enrollment: Apple supports trader details for both individual and organization accounts. Do not treat converting to an organization as a universal prerequisite to trader declaration or a worldwide store blocker.

Confirm the intended territories, legal seller identity, account access and trader status in each console. Do not infer Google's deadline or verification state from Apple's policy. A domain role mailbox and working mail authentication are operational recommendations, not a substitute for verification.

Sources: [Apple trader requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/), [Apple enforcement date](https://developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/?id=02172025a).
