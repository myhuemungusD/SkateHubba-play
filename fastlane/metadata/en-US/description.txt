SkateHubba is real-time multiplayer S.K.A.T.E. — the classic trick-matching game, played against real skaters wherever they are.

HOW IT WORKS
• Challenge another skater to a game of S.K.A.T.E.
• Set a trick: film it in the app and send it.
• Your opponent has to match it on camera — or they take a letter.
• First to spell S.K.A.T.E. loses. Simple as that.

FILM EVERYTHING
Record your set and match attempts right in the app. Clips are the proof — no arguing about whether the trick was landed.

DISPUTES, SETTLED
Think a match attempt was sketchy? Flag it and let a judge make the call.

FIND SPOTS
Browse the spot map, check out where people are skating, and add your own spots.

TRACK YOUR RECORD
Wins, losses, letters taken and given — your stats follow your profile.

STAY IN THE GAME
Turn-based means no waiting around: get a push notification when it's your move, tap it, and you're back in the game.
