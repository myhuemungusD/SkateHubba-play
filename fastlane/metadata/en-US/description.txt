SkateHubba is S.K.A.T.E. on your phone — the classic trick-matching game, played turn by turn against real skaters wherever they are. No need to be at the same spot or online at the same time.

HOW IT WORKS
• Challenge another skater to a game of S.K.A.T.E.
• Set a trick: film it in the app and send it.
• Your opponent has to match it on camera — or they take a letter.
• First to spell S.K.A.T.E. loses. Simple as that.

FILM EVERYTHING
Game turns are one take, filmed right in the app, so the clip is the proof. The pro camera gives you a cleaner look, and the clip editor lets you trim and polish clips you post to the feed.

PLAY ON YOUR CLOCK
Pick 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days per turn when you start a game. Get a push notification when it's your move, tap it, and you're back in.

DISPUTES, SETTLED
Think a match attempt was sketchy? Add an optional referee when you start a game, or let the community vote on a disputed landing.

HIT THE SPOTS
Browse the spot map, add your own spots and check in when you're actually there. Every check-in stamps your spot passport, legendary spots earn rare badges, and you can see who's skating a spot right now. Each spot has its own group chat.

LEVEL UP
Earn XP for finished games and spot check-ins, and climb 50 levels. Wins, losses and letters follow your profile.

MAKE YOUR PROFILE YOURS
Colors, banner, layout, pinned clips, bio and links.

CLIPS FEED
Landed tricks roll into one feed. Upvote the best ones, and report anything that crosses the line.

VERIFIED PROS
Verified pro skaters have their own profiles, showcase clips and challenges.
