SkateHubba is a game of S.K.A.T.E. played over video. You and another skater take turns from wherever you skate. You don't need to be at the same spot or online at the same time.

<b>How it works</b>
• Call out any skater by their handle.
• Set a trick: film it in the app and send it.
• They film their attempt. If they miss, they take a letter.
• Land their trick and the set is yours.
• Spell S.K.A.T.E. and the game's over.

<b>One take</b>
Game turns are filmed in the app in one continuous take. You can't upload from your camera roll and you can't edit. The clip is the proof.

<b>On your clock</b>
Pick 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days a turn when you start a game.

<b>Calling it</b>
Think a make didn't count? Send it to a community vote, or add an optional referee when you start a game.

<b>Your record</b>
Wins, losses, streaks, and letters given and taken live on your profile. Open the profile of anyone you've played to see your head-to-head and your games against them.

<b>Spot passport</b>
Find spots on the map and check in when you're actually there. Every check-in stamps your spot passport. Keep it public or keep it to yourself.

Go skate. Film it. Spell it out.
