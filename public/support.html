SkateHubba Support

Need help with SkateHubba? Email us and a real person will get back to you, usually within 2 business days.

Email support@skatehubba.com

Report a player or clip

In the app, open the clip or the player's profile and tap Report or Block. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. For anything urgent, email support@skatehubba.com.

Delete your account

Open Settings, scroll to Your data & sign out and tap Delete account. Your profile, clips and game history are removed. See data deletion for details.

Sign-in problems

You can sign in with Google or email, and with Apple on iPhone. If you forgot your password, tap Forgot password on the sign-in screen.
